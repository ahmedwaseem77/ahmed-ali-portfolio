Client FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do we exchange large 4K RAW video files?

We primarily use Google Drive, Frame.io, or Dropbox for fast, seamless cloud synchronization. For uncompressed terabyte-scale cinema rushes, we also support MASV.io high-speed file transfer.

What is your turnaround time?

For short-form reels, turnaround is typically 24 to 48 hours per batch. Real estate showcases are delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Commercial post-production timelines depend on VFX and multi-cam scope, typically 4 to 7 business days.

How do revision rounds work?

Every package includes up to 2 comprehensive revision rounds. We review drafts via Frame.io, allowing you to pause at the exact frame and leave precise notes or drawings directly on screen.

What camera formats do you grade?

We natively grade Sony S-Log3, Canon C-Log, Blackmagic RAW (.braw), REDCODE RAW (.r3d), ARRI LogC, DJI D-Log, and Apple ProRes Log directly in DaVinci Resolve Studio’s 32-bit floating point environment.
